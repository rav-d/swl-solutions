## Metrics & Analytics

### Business KPIs
- Lead conversion rate, win rate, average deal size.
- Revenue growth, retention, expansion (upsell/cross-sell).
- Client satisfaction (CSAT/NPS) and referral rate.

### Product/Website KPIs
- Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS), page load time.
- Traffic channels, bounce rate, time on page, scroll depth.
- CTA click-through, contact form completion, demo/booked calls.

### Analytics Stack
- Google Analytics 4 for traffic and funnels; custom events for CTAs.
- Mixpanel/Amplitude for product analytics and cohorts.
- Search Console for SEO; Hotjar/FullStory for UX insights.
- Sentry for error tracking; LogRocket for session replay.

### Implementation Notes
- Define event schema (names, properties) and document in repo.
- Respect privacy: cookie consent, Do Not Track, data retention policy.
- Dashboards by audience: leadership (revenue), sales (pipeline), delivery (throughput), marketing (acquisition).
