## Business-Focused Features & Integrations

### Revenue & Operations
- Pricing tiers and service packages (starter, growth, enterprise).
- Online booking and payments (Stripe); invoicing and receipts.
- Subscription management for retainers and support plans.

### CRM & Sales
- Lead capture with enriched context (budget, timeline, goals).
- CRM integrations: HubSpot, Pipedrive, or Salesforce.
- Automated follow-ups, reminders, and pipeline stages.

### Client Success
- Client portal: milestones, file sharing, approvals, and messaging.
- Satisfaction surveys (CSAT/NPS) and referral program tracking.

### Marketing Automation
- Email campaigns (Mailchimp/ConvertKit) with segmentation.
- Content calendar and social scheduling; UTM tracking.

### Integrations
- Project management: Jira/Asana/Trello/Monday.
- Communication: Slack/Teams notifications.
- Accounting: QuickBooks/Xero/FreshBooks.
