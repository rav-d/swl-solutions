## New Feature Ideas (Expansion)

### Portfolio & Case Studies
- Project gallery with tag filtering (web, mobile, backend, AI, cloud).
- Deep-dive case study template: context, goals, constraints, solution, screenshots, stack, metrics.
- Before/after comparisons, embedded demos, and GitHub/live links.

### Lead Gen & Sales
- Smart intake form with dynamic questions by selected services.
- Cost estimator (non-binding) to educate clients on scope and trade-offs.
- Downloadable capability deck (PDF) gated by email.

### Client Experience
- Client portal MVP: authenticated area with project status, milestones, files, and messages.
- Shared notes and approvals workflow for deliverables.

### Content & Authority
- Blog with categories (engineering, design, product, cloud, AI).
- Tutorials and technical write-ups; newsletter signup and RSS feed.
- Speaking/events and awards page.

### Internationalization
- i18n support (e.g., English, Korean, Chinese) with language switcher and localized content.

### Communication
- Live chat (e.g., Intercom/Drift), meeting scheduler (Calendly), and call-to-action ribbons.
