## Text Styling Guidelines

### **Typography**:
- **Primary Font**: Inter (matches website)
- **Weights**: 400 (regular), 600 (semibold), 700 (bold), 900 (black)
- **Colors**: White (#FFFFFF), Blue (#3671E9), Dark Blue (#0D0D2B)

### **Animation Effects**:
- **Fade In/Out**: 0.5-1 second transitions
- **Slide In**: From left/right with smooth easing
- **Scale**: Subtle zoom effects for emphasis
- **Glow**: Blue glow for key highlights

### **Positioning**:
- **Center**: Main headlines and CTAs
- **Top**: Company name and branding
- **Bottom**: Subtitles and supporting text
- **Sides**: Supporting elements and metrics

### **Timing**:
- **Text Duration**: 3-5 seconds per overlay
- **Transitions**: 0.5-1 second between text changes
- **Pauses**: 1-2 seconds for key messages
- **Sync**: Align with background video transitions

## Export Specifications

### **Video Format**:
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- **Frame Rate**: 30fps
- **Duration**: 120 seconds (2 minutes)
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264 codec)

### **Text Layer Requirements**:
- **Separate Text Track**: For easy editing
- **Transparent Background**: PNG sequence or video track
- **High Contrast**: Ensure readability over video
- **Responsive**: Test on different screen sizes
